DC Josephson Effect

When I_b < I_c, Cooper pairs tunnel coherently through the barrier without any voltage developing. The supercurrent flows with zero resistance: I_s = I_c · sin(φ) where φ is the phase difference across the junction. This is the DC Josephson effect — a macroscopic quantum phenomenon.
